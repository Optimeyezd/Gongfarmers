About Us

The Treasure Valley Gongfarmers are a crew of old‑school adventurers within the larger Treasure Valley Gamers community. We crawl dungeons, roll funny dice, and test our luck in Dungeon Crawl Classics, Shadowdark, and Old‑School Essentials.

We play at ABU Games in Boise. Sign‑ups for all sessions run through Treasure Valley Gamers, and we keep our own corner for table tales and the (frequent) demise of heroes.
